Updated: 2026-05-14

Mandatory claim levels for every runtime/governance statement:
- VERIFIED
- SELF-ASSERTED
- BLOCKED
- ROADMAP

Rules:
1. VERIFIED requires reproducible evidence path (hash/log/curl output).
2. SELF-ASSERTED may be reported by operator but must include owner + timestamp.
3. BLOCKED must include blocker cause and next action owner.
4. ROADMAP is intent only; cannot be presented as executed state.

Mandatory evidence fields:
- TASK_ID
- COMMIT
- SHA256
- SMOKE_TEST
- ROLLBACK
- STATUS
- CLAIM_LEVEL
- TIMESTAMP
- OWNER
